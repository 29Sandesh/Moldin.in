Straight answers

Questions brands and creators actually ask.

Why work with a small boutique desk instead of a large marketplace? +

Large self-serve platforms hand you thousands of unfiltered profiles and leave you doing all the DMing, shipping, and quality checks. Big agencies charge heavy retainers and hand execution down to junior reps. Because we stay small, you work directly with the operators screening every creator and reviewing every draft.

Why don't you publish a public creator database or fixed rate sheet? +

Public directories get scraped immediately, flooding creators with lowball spam and inviting brand bypassing. Static rate cards also ignore variables like paid ad whitelisting, usage duration, and creative complexity. Instead, we explain our commercial rules openly and share curated shortlists privately per brief.

How small can a first brand collaboration be? +

You can start with a focused pilot of 5 to 12 creators (or a compact UGC creative batch for ads). It gives you real evidence of our curation taste, delivery speed, and content performance before expanding.

Is there really 0% cut taken from creators? +

Yes. Creators receive 100% of their agreed collaboration fee. Our coordination fee is scoped transparently on the brand side as a separate line item—never hidden inside a creator's payout.
