Straight talk

Got questions? Direct answers.

Why use a lean boutique desk over big platforms? +

Marketplaces dump you into thousands of unvetted profiles and leave you doing all the DMing and chasing. Large agencies lock you in retainers. Here, the operators reviewing drafts are the same people curating your shortlist.

Why aren’t full rosters and fixed prices public? +

Open lists get scraped immediately by spammers, and static rate sheets ignore ad whitelisting rights or usage terms. We show our rules upfront and share customized creator shortlists privately once you send a brief.

How small can a first pilot campaign be? +

Start with 5–12 creators or a crisp UGC creative batch. Prove the vibe, speed, and fit before ever committing to larger ongoing drops.

Is MOLDIN really free for creators? +

Yes. 0% fee cut from agreed creator deliverables. Brands cover our coordination scope cleanly as a separate line item.
